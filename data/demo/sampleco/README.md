# SampleCo Demo Dataset

Synthetic diligence document for Executive AI Advisor. SampleCo is a fictional company created for testing and demonstration.

## Company Profile

SampleCo is a fictional $12M ARR B2B SaaS company with 75 employees and a 12-person engineering team. The company is AWS-hosted, has moderate technical debt, incomplete security governance, no formal AI governance, and key-person dependency on the VP Engineering.

SampleCo is preparing for growth equity investment. The dataset is useful for demonstrating a balanced diligence scenario: credible product and architecture, but meaningful governance, security, AI readiness, and operating-model gaps.

## Document List

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `01-executive-summary.md` | Board-level company, investment, and technology risk summary |
| `02-technology-assessment.md` | Architecture, data, scalability, reliability, and technical debt |
| `03-security-assessment.md` | Security controls, incident response, compliance, and AI governance risk |
| `04-engineering-organization.md` | Engineering team model, delivery, key-person risk, and hiring needs |
| `05-ai-readiness.md` | AI opportunity, data readiness, governance gaps, and phased AI plan |
| `06-technology-roadmap.md` | 100-day and 18-month technology maturity roadmap |
| `07-cloud-cost-analysis.md` | AWS spend, unit economics, cost drivers, and optimization plan |

## Intended Demo Use

Use this dataset to test technology diligence reports, board technology briefs, AI governance assessments, risk scoring, 100-day technology plans, and management interview questions for a mid-market B2B SaaS growth equity scenario.

## Suggested Questions

- What are the top technology risks?
- What governance concerns should the board monitor?
- What AI readiness gaps exist?
- What key-person risks exist?
- What should be included in the first 100-day technology plan?
- How should cloud cost and scalability be assessed?

## Expected Risks The System Should Detect

- Moderate technical debt in legacy admin, reporting, integrations, and test coverage.
- Incomplete security governance and limited compliance readiness.
- No formal AI governance despite internal AI usage and planned AI features.
- Key-person dependency on the VP Engineering.
- AWS-hosted multi-tenant SaaS needs stronger operating maturity.
- Cloud cost governance and tenant-level cost attribution need improvement.
