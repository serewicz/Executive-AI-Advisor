# FinTechCo Demo Dataset

Synthetic demo document for Executive AI Advisor. Not based on any real company.

## Company Profile

FinTechCo is a fictional $18M ARR payments and treasury workflow platform with 120 employees and a 25-person engineering team. The platform is AWS-hosted, Kubernetes-based, and uses PostgreSQL, Redis, and Kafka to support payment workflow orchestration, treasury approvals, transaction event processing, and customer operations.

The company is preparing for a Series C or growth equity process. It has meaningful growth momentum, but diligence should surface regulated-fintech operating risk: PCI-DSS scope, privileged access governance, vendor concentration, semi-manual SOC 2 evidence collection, and incomplete AI governance.

## Expected Risks The System Should Detect

- PCI-DSS scope is not fully minimized.
- SOC 2 controls exist but evidence collection remains semi-manual.
- Privileged access reviews are quarterly but inconsistently documented.
- Incident response has not been tested in the last 12 months.
- Vendor concentration exists around the primary payment processor and KYC provider.
- AI use cases are emerging without a formal AI governance board.
- Kubernetes ownership is split across DevOps and platform engineering.
- Key-person risk exists around the principal platform engineer and head of DevOps.
- Cloud spend increased 42 percent year over year.
