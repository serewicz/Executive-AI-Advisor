# Example AI Governance Assessment

Investigation: SampleCo Diligence  
Report Type: AI Governance Assessment  
Scope: Synthetic demo content

## Executive Summary

SampleCo has early AI interest but lacks a complete governance operating model. The main risks are unclear use case ownership, incomplete data handling controls, limited model/output evaluation, lack of human review standards, and immature cost and auditability practices. The next step is to make AI adoption visible, governed, measurable, and tied to business outcomes.

Overall AI Governance Maturity: **Medium**  
Risk Rating: **Yellow**  
Confidence: **Medium**

| Category | Maturity | Risk | Business Impact | Owner | Timeline | Success Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Use-Case Clarity | Medium | Yellow | AI work may fragment without approved business owners and success criteria. | CTO | Days 31-60 | Approved AI use case inventory includes owner, outcome, risk tier, and review date. |
| Business Outcome Alignment | Medium | Yellow | AI pilots may consume time and budget without measurable value. | CEO | Days 31-60 | Each AI initiative has an executive sponsor and business metric. |
| Data Privacy and Security | Low | Red | Sensitive data could be exposed to tools or models without clear controls. | CISO / Security Lead | Days 1-30 | AI workflows have documented data classifications and prohibited data rules. |
| Model / Output Evaluation | Medium | Yellow | Unsupported model outputs could affect quality, trust, or customer-facing decisions. | CTO | Days 31-60 | Outputs are tested against documented quality and safety criteria. |
| Human-in-the-Loop Controls | Medium | Yellow | High-impact AI workflows may lack review or escalation. | Product Leader | Days 31-60 | Named reviewers and exception paths are documented. |
| Cost Management | Medium | Yellow | Usage can scale without budget ownership or spend alerts. | Finance | Days 31-60 | AI spend dashboard and threshold alerts reviewed monthly. |
| Auditability | Low | Red | Leadership may lack evidence for AI decisions, approvals, and exceptions. | CISO / Security Lead | Days 1-30 | Prompts, outputs, approvals, and exceptions are traceable. |
| Vendor / Model Dependency | Medium | Yellow | Vendor concentration may create continuity, cost, and data residency risk. | CTO | Days 31-60 | Critical vendors have risk review and fallback options. |
| Compliance and Policy Readiness | Medium | Yellow | AI usage may outpace privacy, security, and sector obligations. | Board | Days 31-60 | AI policies map to governance obligations and evidence artifacts. |

## Recommended Next Steps

- Approve an AI use case inventory and risk-tiering process.
- Define prohibited data handling rules for AI tools.
- Establish model/output evaluation standards before operational use.
- Require human review for high-impact workflows.
- Add AI spend and usage visibility to the monthly operating review.
