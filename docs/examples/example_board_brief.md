# Example Board Brief

Investigation: SampleCo Diligence  
Report Type: Board Brief  
Scope: Synthetic demo content

## Executive Summary

SampleCo appears investable from a technology perspective, but the board should treat security governance, key-person dependency, cloud cost visibility, technical debt, and AI governance as operating risks requiring near-term management attention. The recommended board posture is not to pause growth, but to require clear ownership, evidence, and 30/60/90-day progress reporting.

## Top 5 Technology Risks

1. **Security governance is incomplete.**  
   Business impact: customer trust, enterprise readiness, and incident response may be exposed if controls remain informal.  
   Recommended action: require a security owner, control inventory, remediation dates, and exception reporting.

2. **Key-person dependency is concentrated in VP Engineering.**  
   Business impact: continuity, integration, and scaling risk increase if critical system knowledge remains undocumented.  
   Recommended action: document critical workflows and assign backup owners.

3. **Cloud cost visibility is limited.**  
   Business impact: margin pressure may grow as usage scales without product, customer, or environment-level attribution.  
   Recommended action: create a FinOps dashboard and monthly operating review.

4. **Technical debt affects delivery predictability.**  
   Business impact: roadmap confidence and customer commitments may be harder to protect.  
   Recommended action: prioritize technical debt by business impact and delivery drag.

5. **AI governance is not formalized.**  
   Business impact: AI adoption may create data privacy, cost, vendor, and auditability risks.  
   Recommended action: approve an AI use case inventory, review process, and data handling policy.

## Key Decisions Needed

- Should management establish a formal security and AI governance cadence before broad AI adoption?
- Which technology risks require board reporting at every meeting?
- Does current leadership capacity match the required remediation timeline?

## Questions for Management

- What technology risks could materially affect growth, customer trust, integration, or margin?
- What evidence will management provide in 30 days?
- Which critical workflows lack backup ownership?
