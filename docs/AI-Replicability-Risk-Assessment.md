# AI Replicability Risk Assessment

The AI Replicability Risk Assessment helps leadership teams evaluate whether AI-enabled capabilities create durable competitive advantage or can be easily replicated by competitors.

The assessment is designed for CTOs, boards, investors, operating partners, and diligence teams reviewing defensibility, proprietary data, workflow advantage, knowledge advantage, operational maturity, and governance.

## What It Evaluates

- Defensibility of AI-enabled capabilities
- Proprietary data advantage
- Workflow advantage and customer switching costs
- Knowledge advantage and institutional expertise
- Operational maturity and evidence of repeatable execution
- Governance, auditability, and regulatory barriers

## Outputs

- Overall Replicability Risk: red, yellow, or green
- Executive assessment
- Replicability drivers
- Defensibility factors
- Competitive barriers
- Evidence and missing evidence
- Management questions
- Board discussion points
- Recommendations
- 90-day improvement plan

The assessment uses evidence from the active investigation workspace and includes citations, confidence, and limitations.
