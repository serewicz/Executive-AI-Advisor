# Example Technology Risk Scorecard

Investigation: SampleCo Diligence  
Report Type: Technology Risk Scorecard  
Scope: Synthetic demo content

## Executive Summary

SampleCo shows moderate technology risk for a growth-equity stage B2B SaaS company. The most material concerns are incomplete security governance, key-person dependency, technical debt, and limited AI governance. These issues do not block investment by themselves, but they require named owners, board-visible milestones, and measurable operating controls.

| Category | Status | Confidence | Business Impact | Owner | Timeline | Success Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture | Yellow | Medium | Scaling pressure may increase delivery friction if modernization work remains informal. | CTO | Days 31-60 | Architecture decision log and modernization backlog approved. |
| Security | Red | Medium | Incomplete governance increases customer trust, compliance, and incident response risk. | CISO / Security Lead | Days 1-30 | Critical controls have owners, due dates, and exception reporting. |
| AI Governance | Yellow | Medium | AI experimentation could create data handling, cost, and auditability gaps. | CTO | Days 31-60 | AI use case inventory and review process approved. |
| Data Handling | Yellow | Medium | Sensitive data flows are not fully mapped for AI and reporting workflows. | CISO / Security Lead | Days 31-60 | Critical data flows and access controls documented. |
| Cloud / Infrastructure | Yellow | Medium | Cloud cost growth may pressure margin without product-level visibility. | Finance | Days 31-60 | Cloud cost dashboard reviewed monthly. |
| Delivery Predictability | Yellow | Medium | Technical debt and unclear prioritization may reduce roadmap confidence. | VP Engineering | Days 31-60 | Cycle time, defect rate, and release cadence tracked. |
| Key-Person Risk | Red | Medium | VP Engineering dependency creates continuity and integration risk. | CEO | Days 1-30 | Backup owners documented for critical workflows. |
| Technical Debt | Yellow | Medium | Debt may slow delivery and increase operational risk during growth. | VP Engineering | Days 61-100 | Debt backlog prioritized by business impact. |
| Compliance Readiness | Yellow | Low | Evidence is incomplete for formal compliance review. | CISO / Security Lead | Days 31-60 | Compliance evidence register mapped to owners. |

## Board Questions

- Which red risks could materially affect growth, customer trust, or integration readiness?
- What evidence will management provide at the next board meeting?
- Which owners have authority and budget to reduce the highest-risk items?
