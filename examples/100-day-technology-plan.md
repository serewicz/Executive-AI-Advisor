# Example 100-Day Technology Plan

Investigation: SampleCo Diligence  
Plan Type: Growth Equity  
Scope: Synthetic demo content

## Executive Summary

The first 100 days should reduce execution risk while preserving growth momentum. The plan prioritizes security ownership, key-person risk reduction, delivery visibility, cloud cost management, and AI governance. The operating principle is simple: every material risk needs an owner, date, evidence artifact, and board-visible success metric.

## Days 1-30: Assess and Stabilize

| Priority | Action | Owner | Expected Outcome | Success Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | Assign owners for security governance gaps and critical remediation items. | CISO / Security Lead | Security accountability is visible to management and board. | Critical controls have owners, due dates, and exception reporting. |
| High | Document critical workflows owned by VP Engineering and name backup owners. | CEO / VP Engineering | Key-person dependency begins to reduce. | Backup owners assigned for deployment, incident response, architecture, and vendor relationships. |
| Medium | Establish AI use case inventory and initial approval criteria. | CTO | AI experimentation becomes governed. | Inventory includes owner, business outcome, data sensitivity, and review date. |

## Days 31-60: Align and Prioritize

| Priority | Action | Owner | Expected Outcome | Success Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Create cloud cost dashboard by environment, product, and workload. | Finance / CTO | Margin and scaling risks become visible. | Monthly cloud cost review adopted. |
| Medium | Prioritize technical debt by customer impact, revenue impact, and delivery drag. | VP Engineering | Roadmap tradeoffs become explicit. | Debt backlog approved with impact and target dates. |

## Days 61-100: Execute and Measure

| Priority | Action | Owner | Expected Outcome | Success Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | Publish technology operating review covering delivery, security, cloud cost, and AI governance. | CTO | Board and management see execution progress. | Operating review delivered monthly with trend metrics. |
| Medium | Validate AI governance controls with one approved pilot. | Product Leader / CTO | Governance process is tested before broader rollout. | Pilot has documented evaluation, human review, cost tracking, and data handling evidence. |

## Board Checkpoints

- Day 30: Are owners assigned for red risks?
- Day 60: Are security, cost, delivery, and AI governance metrics visible?
- Day 90: Is technology execution improving against measurable indicators?

This example is synthetic and intended to demonstrate the structure of an Executive AI Advisor output.
