# Example AI Governance Assessment

Investigation: SampleCo Diligence<br>
Report Type: AI Governance Assessment<br>
Scope: Synthetic demo content

## Executive Summary

SampleCo has early AI interest but lacks a complete governance operating model. The main risks are unclear use case ownership, incomplete data handling controls, limited model/output evaluation, lack of human review standards, and immature cost and auditability practices. The next step is to make AI adoption visible, governed, measurable, and tied to business outcomes.

## Maturity Rating

Overall AI Governance Maturity: **Medium**

Risk Rating: **Yellow**<br>

Risk: **Yellow**<br>

Risk: **Yellow**<br>

Maturity: **Low**<br>
Risk: **Red**<br>

Owner: CISO / Security Lead<br>
Timeline: Days 1-30<br>

## Vendor Dependency

Maturity: **Medium**<br>
Risk: **Yellow**<br>

Owner: CTO<br>
Timeline: Days 31-60<br>

## Compliance Readiness

Maturity: **Medium**<br>
Risk: **Yellow**<br>
Business impact: AI usage may outpace privacy, security, and sector obligations.<br>
Owner: Board<br>
Timeline: Days 31-60<br>
Success metric: AI policies map to governance obligations and evidence artifacts.

## Recommended Actions

- Approve an AI use case inventory and risk-tiering process.
- Define prohibited data handling rules for AI tools.
- Establish model/output evaluation standards before operational use.
- Require human review for high-impact workflows.
- Add AI spend and usage visibility to the monthly operating review.
